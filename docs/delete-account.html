删除账号

适用于 AscentRoam Android 应用（com.ascentroam.app）　·　最近更新：2026 年 11 月 4 日

两种方式任选其一：在应用内删除（立即生效），或发邮件请求删除（不用安装应用）。 两种方式删除的数据完全一致。

方式一：在应用内删除（推荐）

  1. 打开 AscentRoam，进入底部导航的「我的」。
  2. 点右上角的齿轮图标进入设置。
  3. 滑到最底部，在「账号」分区点「删除账号」。
  4. 确认框会列出将被删除的内容，点「删除」。

删除后账号立刻失效，界面回到未登录状态。只有登录状态下才会显示「账号」分区——没有账号就没有可删的东西。

方式二：发邮件请求删除（不用安装应用）

用注册时使用的邮箱发信到 support@ascentroam.com， 主题写「删除账号」，正文注明你的注册邮箱即可。我们会在 7 个工作日内处理并回复确认。 为防止他人冒名删除，必要时我们会要求你从注册邮箱回信确认。

会删除什么

  • 账号本身：邮箱、昵称、密码哈希（不可逆）、Google 绑定关系。
  • 云端数据：学习进度、海拔、连续天数、成就徽章、复习队列、头像。
  • 这台设备上的数据：本机进度、徽章、头像文件、本地用户名。

会保留什么

  • 与账号无关的本机偏好：界面主题、界面语言、当前课程轨道、首启引导是否已完成。
  • 加密灾备备份最多保留 30 天，到期自动删除；备份仅用于灾难恢复。
  • 法律法规要求留存的记录（如有）会按法定期限保留。

只想清空本机数据？

不必删除账号：退出登录即可停止同步；本机记录可以在「设置 → 数据 → 重置学习进度」里清空， 或直接卸载应用。卸载会清除这台设备上的一切本机数据，但不会影响云端的账号与进度。

本页仅为说明用途。若你所在的司法辖区有额外要求（如 GDPR「被遗忘权」），请通过上面的邮箱联系我们。

Delete your account

For the AscentRoam Android app (com.ascentroam.app)　·　Last updated: 4 November 2026

Two ways, pick either: delete in the app (immediate), or request deletion by email (no need to install anything). Both remove exactly the same data.

Option 1 — in the app (recommended)

  1. Open AscentRoam and go to Profile in the bottom bar.
  2. Tap the gear icon in the top right to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account in the Account section.
  4. The confirmation dialog lists what will be removed — tap Delete.

The account stops working immediately and the app returns to the signed-out state. The Account section only appears while you are signed in — without an account there is nothing to delete.

Option 2 — request by email (no app required)

Email support@ascentroam.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete account". We will process it within 7 business days and confirm by reply. To stop someone else from deleting your account, we may ask you to confirm from the registered address.

What gets deleted

  • The account itself: email, nickname, password hash (irreversible), Google link if any.
  • Cloud data: progress, altitude, streak, badges, review queue, avatar.
  • Data on this device: local progress, badges, the avatar file, the local display name.

What is kept

  • Device preferences unrelated to the account: theme, interface language, current track, whether onboarding is done.
  • Encrypted disaster-recovery backups are kept for at most 30 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Records we are legally required to retain (if any) are kept for the statutory period.

Only want to clear local data?

You do not have to delete the account: signing out stops syncing, and Settings → Data → Reset learning progress clears the records on this device. Uninstalling also wipes everything stored locally, but leaves the account and cloud progress untouched.

This page is descriptive only. If your jurisdiction imposes additional requirements (for example the GDPR right to erasure), please contact us at the address above.
