先说清楚一件事

这是一款教学应用，不是登山向导，也不能替代实地培训。 山上的判断要靠你自己、你的队伍和你的经验。App 能做的，是把该知道的东西讲明白。

离线优先

所有课程与题库都内置在 App 里，没有网络也能完整学习、做题、看技能书。 账号与云同步是可选的：登录之后，换设备可以恢复进度，多台设备之间会自动合并。 不登录，你的学习记录就只留在这台设备上。

账号与数据

我们只收邮箱、昵称、头像和你主动同步的学习记录。不收集位置、通讯录，也没有广告 SDK。

One thing to be clear about

This is a teaching app, not a mountain guide, and it does not replace hands-on instruction. Judgment on the mountain comes from you, your team and your experience. What this app can do is explain what you need to know.

Offline first

Every lesson and question ships inside the app — you can study, practise and read the handbooks with no connection at all. Accounts and cloud sync are optional: sign in and your progress follows you to a new device and merges across devices. Without an account, your progress stays on this device only.

Account and data

We collect an email address, a nickname, an optional avatar, and the learning progress you choose to sync. No location, no contacts, no advertising SDKs.
