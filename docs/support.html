支持 AscentRoam

AscentRoam 是一个独立开发、免费开放的户外学习项目：没有广告，没有付费解锁， 所有课程、题库与技能书对所有用户一样开放。

这笔钱是什么

赞助完全自愿，用来支持项目继续开发和维护。它不是购买，也不是订阅。

  • 不会解锁任何功能、课程、题库或技能书
  • 不会获得专属徽章、称号、赞助者名单或优先支持
  • 不会改变你在 App 里的任何体验

换句话说：赞助前后，你用的还是同一个 AscentRoam。 所有用户始终享有相同的功能与内容。

Support AscentRoam

AscentRoam is an independently built, free and open outdoor learning project: no ads, no paid unlocks, and every lesson, question and handbook open to everyone.

What this money is

Sponsorship is entirely voluntary and supports continued development and maintenance. It is not a purchase and not a subscription.

  • It does not unlock any feature, lesson, question bank or handbook
  • It does not bring a badge, a title, a supporter list or priority support
  • It does not change anything about your experience in the app

In other words: before and after sponsoring, you are using the same AscentRoam. Every user always gets the same features and content.

How to support

Two options — pick one, or neither. Any amount, one time, no ongoing commitment.

Afdian
Convenient from mainland China; WeChat and Alipay.
Go to Afdian
Ko-fi
Convenient from abroad; no platform fee, payment processing fees apply.
Go to Ko-fi
Payment happens on those sites, not in the app. The app shows no prices, products or payment forms, and does not record who has sponsored.

Where it goes

Servers and domain, terrain maps and illustrations, multilingual proofreading. There is no commercial plan for the project; sponsorship simply keeps these going.

Questions

Will sponsorship show on my profile

No. The app shows no sponsorship status and has no supporter list.

Can I get a refund

Sponsorship is a voluntary gift and is not refundable in principle. If it was a mistake, email support@ascentroam.com.

What if I don't sponsor

Nothing changes. All features and content are open to every user regardless.
